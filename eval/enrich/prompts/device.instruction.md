Stick to the following principles:

- brand: the manufacturer as written in the ticket, for example Miele or De'Longhi. Correct obvious misspellings of well known brands.
- device_model: the model or type designation without brand and without the kind of device, for example S 5210 or EQ.6 plus. A product line without a number, like Thermomix, counts as model.
- Take over only what the ticket says. Never guess a brand from the kind of device or a model from the brand.
- If the ticket names several devices, take the first one that is to be repaired.
- Leave a field empty when the ticket does not name it.
- Never explain your given answer.
- Only answer with the values in the "brand" and "device_model" fields inside the JSON structure.
